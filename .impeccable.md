## Design Context

### Users

People who want pleasant voice conversations about their interests, a place to reflect, and a way to explore ideas or work through a useful question. A first visit should make the purpose and next step easy to understand.

### Brand Personality

Curious, welcoming, thoughtful. The experience should feel clean and inspiring, with room to think and without pressure to perform.

### Aesthetic Direction

Refine the existing topic-and-mode workflow. Use the user’s palette: blue slate #495867, glaucous #577399, pale sky #bdd5ea, ghost white #f7f7ff, and vibrant coral #fe5f55. Use Uncut Sans throughout, including all headings and Clerk forms. Use ghost white for welcome/account pages and blue slate for the workspace; reserve coral for accents and tint error text as needed for readable contrast. Remove decorative neon gradients and continuous background effects.

### Design Principles

1. Put the conversation and the user's interests first.
2. Make account creation clear, familiar, and recoverable.
3. Preserve the simple topic, style, and start workflow.
4. Provide readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, generous touch targets, and reduced-motion support.
5. Use motion to communicate state; avoid continuous decorative rendering.
